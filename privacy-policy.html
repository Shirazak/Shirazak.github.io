Privacy Policy

App: BundleDesk: SplitAPK Installer (com.nativeinvaders.bundledesk)
Developer: Native Invaders
Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Native Invaders ("we", "our", or "us") built BundleDesk: SplitAPK Installer as an ad-supported Android utility app with an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the app.

1. Local File Processing (No File Uploads)

BundleDesk only accesses Android package files (.apk, .apks, .xapk, .apkm, and .zip) that you explicitly choose using Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework). All archive inspection, device ABI compatibility checks, SHA-256 verification, and split-APK staging happen entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, or transmit your selected files or installed app lists to any external server.

2. Permissions Used

  • Request Install Packages (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES): Used solely to initiate Android's native system installer for APK and split-APK bundles you manually select.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used by Google AdMob to serve ads and by Google Play Billing to process optional ad-removal purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of BundleDesk displays banner and pre-install interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and share limited technical information — such as your device's Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, app interaction events, and crash/performance diagnostics — to deliver and measure ads.

You can review Google's data practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and opt out of personalized ads anytime in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, or permanently disable all ads inside BundleDesk via the optional one-time Remove Ads upgrade.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional lifetime ad removal is processed securely by Google Play. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details, billing address, or Google account credentials.

5. Data Storage & Deletion

BundleDesk does not require user accounts or cloud sign-in. Your local package history and app preferences are stored strictly on your device and can be cleared at any time inside the app or by clearing app storage / uninstalling BundleDesk.

6. Children's Privacy

BundleDesk is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
